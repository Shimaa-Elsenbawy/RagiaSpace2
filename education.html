WHY ASTRONOMY?

Understand The World Beyond Earth

Astronomy helps us understand where we come from, how the universe works, and what exists beyond our own planet.

At Raqia, we believe that learning about space should be clear, interesting, and accessible to everyone. Our educational content takes you from basic concepts to deeper scientific ideas.
