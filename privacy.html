About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or interact with the Raqia website.

We aim to keep our approach simple, transparent, and respectful of the people who use our platform.

01

Information We May Receive

Depending on the features available on the website, you may choose to provide information such as your name, email address, or message when contacting the Raqia team.

We only want to request information that is relevant to the purpose for which you provide it.

02

How Information May Be Used

Information provided through the website may be used to respond to questions, provide requested information, improve the user experience, and communicate about relevant Raqia activities.

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes unrelated to the reason it was provided.

03

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Websites may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand how pages are used, or improve website performance.

If analytics, authentication, or other services are added to Raqia in the future, this section should be updated to describe those services clearly.

04

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

No online system can guarantee complete security, so users should also take care when sharing personal information online.

05

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on the information collected and the applicable laws, you may have rights relating to accessing, correcting, or requesting deletion of personal information.

You can contact us to ask questions about information connected to your interactions with the website.

06

Contact Us About Privacy

If you have a question about this policy or about how information is handled, please contact the Raqia team.
