OUR RESEARCH

From Questions To Discovery

Astronomy is built around questions: How do stars form? How do galaxies change? Are there other worlds like our own?

Research helps us turn those questions into measurable evidence. Scientists observe distant objects, collect data, analyze their results, and use what they learn to build better models of the universe.

RESEARCH METHOD

From Observation To Discovery

Scientific research can be explained through a simple process: observe, collect evidence, analyze the results, and share what was learned.

01

Observe

Study celestial objects using telescopes and scientific observations.

02

Collect

Gather measurements, images, and other useful scientific data.

03

Analyze

Compare the evidence and search for patterns that explain what we observe.

04

Share

Communicate results so that other people can learn, discuss, and build on them.
